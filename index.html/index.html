Letters to Myself ♡

Three letters for three versions of me.

🌷

To My Past Self

For the person I used to be.

Dear past me, thank you for being strong even when things were difficult and for continuing to move forward despite the challenges you faced. I know there were moments when you felt confused, made mistakes, or doubted yourself, but every experience helped shape the person I am today. I'm proud of you for never giving up, and I hope you know that you did better than you sometimes believed. ♡
🎀

To My Present Self

For the person I am today.

Dear me, I know I'm still learning, growing, and figuring out what I truly want in life, and that's okay. I hope I learn to trust myself more, stop being too hard on myself, and appreciate the little things that make me happy. I may not have everything figured out yet, but I'm proud of myself for trying, and I will keep moving forward one step at a time. ♡
💌

To My Future Self

For the person I have yet to become.

Dear future me, I hope you're living a life that makes you happy and proud of everything you've worked for. I hope you've achieved your dreams, made beautiful memories, explored wonderful places, and surrounded yourself with people who genuinely care about you. No matter where life takes you, please remember the person you once were, the dreams you carried, and the courage it took to become who you are today. ♡

A Gentle Reminder for the Person I Am Becoming

I don't need to have everything figured out right now. I am allowed to grow at my own pace, make mistakes, change my dreams, and begin again whenever I need to. I deserve the same patience, love, and kindness that I give to others, and I will always be a work in progress worth believing in. 🌷
